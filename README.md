# Hi, I'm Karuna Reddy

I am a data scientist and statistician at the University of Auckland, where I am Deputy Director of Data Science and a Professional Teaching Fellow in the School of Computer Science.

My work covers survey sampling, optimum stratification, distribution fitting, clustering and applied machine learning. I like turning new statistical methods into practical, well-tested open-source tools.
